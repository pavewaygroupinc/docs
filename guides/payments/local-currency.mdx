title: "Local Currency Handling & FX"
description: "Accept payments in local African currencies with automatic FX conversion and unified merchant settlement."
---

Pursar is engineered to remove the friction of African currency fragmentation and cross-border foreign exchange. You can price products in your preferred reporting currency (e.g. `USD`, `EUR`, or `XAF`), while allowing your customers to pay in their local native currency across Central, West, and East Africa. Pursar handles the real-time FX calculation, payment routing, and settlement ledger automatically.

---

## Supported Currencies Matrix

Pursar provides native clearing and settlement across the following regional currencies:

| Currency Code | Monetary Zone / Country | Primary Payment Rails | Zero Decimal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **XAF** | CEMAC Zone (Cameroon, Gabon, Chad, Congo, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea) | MTN MoMo, Orange Money, Express Union | Yes (integer amounts) |
| **XOF** | UEMOA Zone (Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger, Benin, Togo, Guinea-Bissau) | Moov Africa, Orange Money, Wave | Yes (integer amounts) |
| **NGN** | Nigeria | NIP Bank Transfer, Cards | No (supports kobo) |
| **GHS** | Ghana | MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo | No (supports pesewas) |
| **KES** | Kenya | M-Pesa, Airtel Money | No (supports cents) |
| **UGX** | Uganda | MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money | Yes |
| **RWF** | Rwanda | MTN Mobile Money, AirtelTigo | Yes |
| **USD** | International | Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal | No (supports cents) |
| **EUR** | Eurozone / International | Visa, Mastercard, SEPA, PayPal | No (supports cents) |

## Automatic Real-Time FX Conversion

When creating a Checkout Session or Payment Intent, you can specify your base price in USD or EUR, and Pursar will compute the exact local currency payable amount using guaranteed interbank mid-market exchange rates.

### Example: Charging $25 USD for a SaaS Subscription

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.viarosandbox.com/v1/checkout/sessions \
  -u sk_test_live_secret_key_12345: \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 2500,
    "currency": "USD",
    "customer_name": "Amara Diallo",
    "customer_email": "amara@example.com",
    "description": "SaaS Starter Monthly Plan ($25.00)",
    "merchant_reference": "SUB_USD_101",
    "payment_method_types": ["mobile_money", "card", "paypal"]
  }'
```

When Amara opens the hosted checkout screen from Cameroon or Côte d'Ivoire:
1. Pursar detects their local IP or customer preference.
2. Pursar calculates the exact local currency conversion (e.g. `15,250 XAF` at 610 XAF/USD).
3. The customer authorizes `15,250 XAF` via MTN MoMo or Orange Money.
4. Your Pursar Balance is credited with the exact net USD equivalent (or settled into your multi-currency wallet).

## Multi-Currency Wallets & Settlement Options

Pursar gives merchants total control over settlement currency preferences:

1. **Auto-Convert to Base Currency**: All incoming funds from XAF, XOF, GHS, KES, NGN are automatically converted at capture time and settled into your primary business wallet (e.g. `XAF` or `USD`).
2. **Multi-Currency Hold**: Maintain separate sub-wallets in `XAF`, `XOF`, `USD`, and `EUR` to avoid FX conversion costs when paying local vendors, staff, or recurring payouts.

You can inspect your live currency balances at any time via the [Balance API](/api-reference/balance/get-balance) or the [Wallets API](/api-reference/wallets/list-wallets):

```bash
curl -X GET https://api.viarosandbox.com/v1/balance \
  -u sk_test_live_secret_key_12345:
```
