Pursar operates an ultra-transparent pricing engine with zero hidden setup costs, monthly maintenance fees, or foreign exchange surcharges. You can configure whether fees are absorbed by your business or passed to your customer on a per-transaction basis.

## Fee Bearer Configuration

Every charge and Checkout Session supports the `fee_bearer` parameter:

| `fee_bearer` | Mechanism | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`merchant`** (Default) | Transaction fees are deducted from your gross payout. Your customer pays exactly the advertised price (e.g. customer pays 25,000 XAF; merchant receives 24,700 XAF net). | Premium e-commerce, SaaS, retail where round pricing builds trust. |
| **`customer`** | Processing fees are added on top of your base price at checkout. The customer pays the fee; your business receives 100% of the requested amount (e.g. customer pays 25,300 XAF; merchant receives 25,000 XAF net). | Invoices, B2B wholesale, utility bills, school tuition. |

### Example Request Setting Fee Bearer

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.viarosandbox.com/v1/charges/mobile-money \
  -u sk_test_live_secret_key_12345: \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 25000,
    "currency": "XAF",
    "phone": "+237671234567",
    "customer_name": "Amara Diallo",
    "customer_email": "amara@example.com",
    "description": "Tuition Payment",
    "merchant_reference": "TUI_104",
    "fee_bearer": "customer"
  }'
```
