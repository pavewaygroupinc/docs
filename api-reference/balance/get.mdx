---
title: "Retrieve Balance"
api: "GET /balance"
description: "Retrieves the current available and pending balances on your account across supported currencies."
---

Retrieves the current financial balance of your Pursar account. The balance is broken down into:
- **Available**: Funds that have cleared and are immediately eligible for automated or manual payout.
- **Pending**: Funds from recently processed charges that are awaiting clearing.

---

url = "https://api.viarosandbox.com/v1/balance"
auth = ("sk_test_uLsofsae3tB9Izwgeuy0Mxbh4JIxUPSG", "")
