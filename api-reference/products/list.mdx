---
title: "List Products"
api: "GET /products"
description: "Returns a list of products in your catalog."
---

Products describe the specific goods or digital services you offer to your customers. You can use products in conjunction with Checkout, Payment Links, Invoicing, and Subscriptions.

---

### Query Parameters

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="10">
  A limit on the number of objects to be returned, between `1` and `100`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="active" type="boolean">
  Only return products that are active or inactive.
</ParamField>
