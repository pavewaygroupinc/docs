---
title: "Customers Overview"
description: "Manage customer profiles, saved payment methods, and transaction histories across Pursar."
---

The Customers API allows you to create and manage customer profiles. Customer objects let you track recurring billing, saved payment methods, and lifetime transaction histories for individual shoppers and business clients.

### Key Capabilities

1. **One-Click Payments**: Attach saved Mobile Money phone numbers or Card tokens for fast re-billing.
2. **Subscriptions**: Link recurring subscription plans and automated invoice billing to a customer ID.
3. **Financial Tracking**: View all charges, refunds, and invoices associated with a specific customer.

---

### Authentication

Pursar follows the Stripe-standard HTTP Basic Authentication format (`-u sk_test_...:`), Bearer tokens, or `x-api-key`.
