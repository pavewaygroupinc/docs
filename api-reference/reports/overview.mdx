Pursar Reporting provides downloadable, audit-ready financial statements for accounting reconciliation, tax filing, and auditing.

Reports can be exported in CSV and JSON formats, with line-item breakdowns of:
- Gross payment volumes
- Telco aggregator and card interchange fees
- Net settled amounts
- Refund deductions and chargebacks
- Exchange rate details on cross-border transactions

---

### List Generated Reports

`GET /reports`

Returns a list of financial reports generated for your business.
